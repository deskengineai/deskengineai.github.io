Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Desk Engine AI ("Desk Engine AI", "we", "us") handles information when you use this website (deskengineai.github.io) and the Desk Engine AI desktop app (the "App"), including information we receive from TikTok when you connect your TikTok account. Desk Engine AI is operated by Nathan Demers, Nephi, Utah, USA.

1. Summary

  • The App uses your TikTok data only to show which account is connected and to upload or post the videos you choose.
  • Your TikTok sign-in and account details are stored on your own computer, not on our servers.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information for advertising.
  • You can disconnect and delete your data at any time.

2. Information we collect

This website. The website has no accounts, forms, advertising, or analytics, and we do not set cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address for security and operation, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The App, when you connect TikTok. After you approve access on TikTok's sign-in page, the App receives:

  • Basic profile information: your TikTok open ID, display name, and avatar (permission user.info.basic).
  • Access and refresh tokens that let the App act on your behalf within the permissions you approved.
  • Posting information returned by TikTok when you prepare a post, such as the privacy options available for your account and the status of an upload (permissions video.upload and video.publish).

Content you choose to post. The video file, caption, privacy setting, and interaction settings (comments, Duet, Stitch) you select for each post.

Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message.

The community. Our community is hosted on Skool. Information you provide there is handled under Skool's Privacy Policy.

3. How we use information

  • To sign you in with TikTok and show which TikTok account is connected.
  • To upload the videos you select to your TikTok inbox as drafts, or post them to your profile, using the settings you choose.
  • To show you the status of your uploads.
  • To answer your emails and provide support.

We do not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than the features you use, and we do not access your other videos, messages, followers, or analytics.

4. Where your information is stored

The App runs on your computer. Your TikTok tokens and basic profile information are stored locally on that computer. Video files are sent from your computer directly to TikTok. We do not operate a server that receives or stores your TikTok data.

5. How we share information

We share information only:

  • With TikTok, to carry out the uploads and posts you request. TikTok handles that information under its own Privacy Policy.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or others.

We never sell or rent personal information.

6. How long we keep information

Tokens and profile information stay on your computer until you disconnect TikTok in the App or uninstall the App. Access tokens also expire on TikTok's schedule. Emails are kept only as long as needed to respond and keep a record of the conversation.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect: click "Disconnect" in the App to delete the saved tokens and profile information from your computer.
  • Revoke access: remove Desk Engine AI from the app permissions in your TikTok account settings at any time.
  • Access or deletion: email us to ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor them.

8. Security

Connections to TikTok use HTTPS. Because your tokens are stored on your own computer, keep that computer protected with a password and up-to-date software. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Children

Our services are intended for adults 18 and older and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If a change affects how we use TikTok data, we will post a notice on this website before it takes effect.
