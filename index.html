Desk Engine AI desktop app

A desktop app that publishes short video tutorials to TikTok. Desk Engine AI uses it to share our lessons, and members of our skool.com community use it to post the short videos they make while building. It runs on your own computer and works only with the TikTok account you connect.

How it works

  1. Connect your TikTok account.

    Click "Connect TikTok". A TikTok sign-in page opens in your browser. You review the permissions and approve them. You can disconnect at any time.

  2. Choose a video.

    Pick a finished video file from your computer and write the caption.

  3. Review before posting.

    The app shows the TikTok account you are posting to. You choose who can view the video and which interactions (comments, Duet, Stitch) are allowed. Nothing is posted until you confirm.

  4. Upload to TikTok.

    The app sends the video file from your computer directly to TikTok, either to your TikTok inbox as a draft to finish in the TikTok app, or posted to your profile, whichever you choose.

  5. Disconnect any time.

    Click "Disconnect" in the app to delete the saved sign-in from your computer, or remove access from your TikTok account's app permissions.

TikTok permissions and why we ask for them

PermissionWhat it lets the app doWhy
user.info.basicRead your TikTok display name and avatarSo you can see which account is connected before you post
video.uploadSend a video to your TikTok inbox as a draftSo you can finish editing and post it yourself in the TikTok app
video.publishPost a video directly to your TikTok profileSo a video you have reviewed and confirmed can be posted in one step

The app never posts without your confirmation, never reads your other videos, messages, or followers, and does not sell or share your data. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
